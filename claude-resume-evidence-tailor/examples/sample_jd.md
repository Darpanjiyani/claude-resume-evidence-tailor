# Junior Analytics Engineer — Crestview Supply Co. (fictional posting)

## About the role
Crestview Supply Co. is looking for a Junior Analytics Engineer to help build the data models behind our finance and operations reporting.

## Responsibilities
- Build and maintain dbt models in Snowflake that turn raw order, inventory and shipment data into clean, documented tables
- Write data tests and documentation for every model
- Partner with finance and operations teams to define metrics and keep them consistent
- Maintain Power BI dashboards built on those models
- Use Git and CI/CD to review and ship changes

## Qualifications
- 1–3 years of experience writing SQL
- Hands-on experience with dbt
- Data modeling experience (star schema, dimensional modeling)
- Familiarity with Git and pull-request workflows

## Nice to have
- Python
- Apache Airflow
- Experience with logistics or supply chain data
